Many homes here are on well water rather than municipal systems, and a significant number of residential properties date back several decades. Well Water and Pump Systems

A large number of Siletz-area properties rely on private wells for their water supply. Button Plumbing holds a pump installation specialist license in addition to our plumbing contractor license, which means we are qualified to work on the full well water system from the pump to the fixtures inside your home.

Common well-related service calls in the Siletz area include low water pressure caused by a failing well pump, waterlogged pressure tanks that need replacement, and sediment or mineral buildup in supply lines. If your well water has started to look cloudy, smell different, or the pressure has dropped, those are signs that something in the system needs attention. We can diagnose the issue and get your water flowing properly again.

Older Homes and Pipe Condition

Many homes in and around Siletz were built in the mid-twentieth century or earlier. If your home still has its original plumbing, it may be running on galvanized steel pipes that have been corroding from the inside for decades. Galvanized pipes restrict flow over time as mineral deposits build up inside them, and eventually they develop leaks. Homes with galvanized supply lines often have noticeably lower water pressure at fixtures throughout the house.

Button Plumbing can assess the condition of your existing piping and recommend targeted repairs or a full repipe depending on the situation. We do not push unnecessary work, but we will tell you honestly what we find and what it means for your home.

Water Heaters, Drains, and General Repair

Beyond the well water and aging pipe work that is common in this area, Button Plumbing handles the full range of residential plumbing service. Water heater repair and replacement is one of our most frequent calls. If your water heater is leaking from the base, producing rusty water, or simply not heating like it used to, we can determine whether a repair will solve the problem or whether it is time for a new unit.

We also handle drain and sewer service, fixture installation, bathroom and kitchen remodel plumbing, and general plumbing repair for any issue that comes up in your home or business.
