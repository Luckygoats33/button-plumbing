Privacy Policy

How Button Plumbing LLC collects, uses, and protects your information.

Effective Date: October 2026

Button Plumbing LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website buttonplumbingoregon.com. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website or contact us through our online forms.

Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us through our website contact forms and service request forms, including:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (if provided)
  • Property address (if provided)
  • Description of plumbing service needed

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as Social Security numbers, financial account information, or health information through our website.

How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is used solely for the purpose of:

  • Responding to your service requests and inquiries
  • Scheduling plumbing service appointments
  • Following up on completed work
  • Communicating with you about your plumbing needs

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use our site. These tools collect anonymous information including:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Browser type and device information
  • Referring website or search terms
  • General geographic location (city or region level)

This information is used to improve our website and does not personally identify you. You can disable cookies through your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

Our website may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics for website traffic analysis
  • Google Fonts for web typography

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing the data they collect. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you provide through our website. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites such as the Oregon CCB, Better Business Bureau, and Google. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
