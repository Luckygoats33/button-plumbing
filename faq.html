Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our plumbing services, areas served, and what to expect.

Services

Button Plumbing provides plumbing repair, water heater repair and replacement, drain and sewer service, plumbing installation for new construction and additions, remodel plumbing for kitchens and bathrooms, and pump system installation and service including well pumps.

Yes. Button Plumbing is licensed for both residential and commercial plumbing work. Whether you need service at your home or your business, we can help.

Yes. We repair, maintain, and replace both tank and tankless water heaters for homes and businesses. If your water heater is not producing hot water, is leaking, or is making unusual noises, call us for an assessment.

Yes. Button Plumbing provides drain and sewer service including clearing blockages, diagnosing slow drains, and repairing drain and sewer lines for residential and commercial properties.

Yes. Button Plumbing is a licensed pump installation specialist. We install, repair, and service well pumps and water pump systems for properties throughout rural Lincoln County.

Yes. We provide complete remodel plumbing services including rough-in work, fixture relocation, new supply and drain lines, and final fixture installation for kitchen and bathroom remodel projects.

Location & Hours

Button Plumbing is based in Siletz, Oregon and serves all of Lincoln County. This includes Siletz, Newport, Lincoln City, Toledo, and Depoe Bay. Contact us to confirm availability for your specific location.

Our regular business hours are Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Call (541) 444-2290 to schedule service during business hours.

Our regular business hours are Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. If you have an urgent plumbing issue, call us at (541) 444-2290 and we will do our best to accommodate your situation as quickly as possible.

Pricing & Process
