About Button Plumbing

A local plumbing company rooted in Siletz, Oregon since December 2003.

Our Story

Built on Honest Work in a Small Town

Button Plumbing LLC was established in December 2003 by David Roy Button in Siletz, Oregon. What started as a one-person operation has grown into a trusted local plumbing company with more than two decades of continuous service to Lincoln County residents and businesses.

David grew up understanding the value of hard work and doing things right the first time. That principle became the foundation of Button Plumbing. When a customer calls, they talk directly to a local plumber who knows the area, understands the plumbing challenges unique to coastal Oregon, and takes personal responsibility for every job.

Siletz is a small community, and in a small community your reputation is everything. Button Plumbing has maintained that reputation through straightforward communication, fair pricing, and quality workmanship on every project, whether it is a simple faucet repair or a complete plumbing remodel.

What We Stand For

Our Approach to Plumbing

Button Plumbing operates on a few simple principles that have kept us in business for more than 20 years.

Straight Talk

We tell you what is wrong, what it will take to fix it, and what it will cost before we start work. If something does not need to be replaced, we will not tell you it does. If a repair is a better option than a replacement, we will say so.

Local Knowledge

Coastal Oregon plumbing has its own set of challenges. From older homes in Siletz to beach properties in Depoe Bay, we understand the conditions that affect pipes, water heaters, and drain systems in this region. We have worked on plumbing in homes and commercial buildings throughout Lincoln County for over two decades, and that experience matters when diagnosing problems.

Quality Over Volume

We are not a franchise. We do not dispatch strangers to your home. When you hire Button Plumbing, you get experienced plumbers who take the time to do the job correctly. We would rather do fewer jobs well than rush through more jobs poorly.

Standing Behind Our Work

Our A+ BBB rating and more than 20 years in business reflect a commitment to making things right. If there is an issue with our work, we address it. That is how you stay in business in a community like Siletz.
